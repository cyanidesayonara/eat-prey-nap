Privacy

Eat Prey Nap collects nothing about you or your cat.

The game

It has no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking, and sends nothing anywhere. Your choices (palette, size, sound and screen calibration) are saved on your own device, or in your browser if you play there. The results of the last session are forgotten when you close it.

The Android app does not ask for internet access.

This website

It sets no cookies and loads nothing from other sites. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub may log visitors' IP addresses for security, as its privacy statement explains.

This page is about the Godot game. The older Flutter app, Trivial Purrsuit, had its own policy.
